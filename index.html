Installing on Android

  • Download the file and tap it to open.
  • If your phone says the install is blocked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source (it may be called Install unknown apps).
  • If Play Protect warns about an unrecognized app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

Installing on Windows

  • Download and open the setup file.
  • If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Follow the steps to finish the install.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Open the free web trial in your browser. It lasts 7 days, allows up to 50 parts, and needs no password or card. The Windows PC and Android apps and the full web version need a subscription.

How do I get the full version?

Download the PC or Android app, send us the Device ID it shows on WhatsApp, and choose a plan. We activate your device.

Which devices does it work on?

A Windows PC, an Android phone, and any web browser. One subscription covers 1 PC and 1 phone, and Google Drive sync keeps the same data on both.

Does it need internet?

Yes. It checks your subscription and the live date and time online when it opens, so keep the internet on.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

The software shows the plans and our contact number. Renew with us and it opens again with all your data in place.

How much does it cost after the trial?

Rs 1,500 per month, Rs 15,000 per year, or Rs 150,000 one time for lifetime use.
